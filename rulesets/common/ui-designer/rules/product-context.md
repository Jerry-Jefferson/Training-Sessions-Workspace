# Product Context

- Derive density, visual expression, navigation, copy tone, and interaction cost from the product, audience, frequency, and environment.
- Preserve an existing design system and reuse established primitives before inventing parallel patterns.
- Operational tools should optimize scanning, comparison, and repeated action; branded or consumer experiences may require stronger expression.
- When visual direction is absent, present distinct options and require a human choice instead of silently creating a house style.
- Design sparse, normal, dense, loading, empty, error, permission, destructive, and recovery states that the task can reach.
